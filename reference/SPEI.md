# SPEI.

A set of functions for computing potential evapotranspiration and
several widely used drought indices including the Standardized
Precipitation-Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI).

## Details

Functions
[`spei`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Drought-indices.md)
and
[`spi`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Drought-indices.md)
are the workhorse of the SPEI library. Other functions, such as
[`kern`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Kernel-functions.md)
and
[`parglo.maxlik`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Generalized-Logistic.md),
are auxiliary low-level functions that will not be used directly by the
typical user. Functions for computing potential evapotranspiration are
provided, too, for helping computing the SPEI. They are:
[`thornthwaite`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Potential-evapotranspiration.md),
[`hargreaves`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Potential-evapotranspiration.md)
and
[`penman`](https://sbegueria.github.io/SPEI/reference/Potential-evapotranspiration.md).
